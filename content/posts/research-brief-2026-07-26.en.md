## Editor's Note

The main thread today: 'open vs closed source' has escalated from a technical debate into a fight over regulation and industry rules — OpenAI and Anthropic are reported to be lobbying Washington to restrict open-weight models (especially China's), while Microsoft, Nvidia, Meta and nearly 200 startups joined forces to defend open source, with the regulatory balance deciding future model distribution and the startup entry bar.
