# Privacy Policy

hackcv is an AI-driven news aggregation and digest tool. We value your privacy.

## What We Do Not Collect

- ❌ No account registration required
- ❌ No personal information such as name, phone number, or email
- ❌ No cookies used for user tracking
- ❌ No personalized advertising

## Information Automatically Collected

When you visit hackcv, our servers may automatically log the following:

- **Access time**
- **Pages visited**
- **Browser type**
- **Approximate location (country/region level only)**

This information is used solely for site operations and statistical analysis, and is never tied to any individual.

## AI Processing

- All news content is collected and summarized automatically by AI
- We do not build user profiles of any person
- AI-generated content is for reference only and does not constitute investment advice

## Third-Party Services

hackcv may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

## Policy Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will announce them on the site homepage.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues or email.
