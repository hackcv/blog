# About hackcv

hackcv is an AI news aggregation and digest site.

## Philosophy

Let AI help filter and summarize information, saving reading time so you can focus on what truly matters.

## Features

- 🤖 AI-powered collection of global AI / tech news
- 📝 AI-generated summaries for quick takeaways
- ⚡ Clean reading experience with zero ad distractions
- 📱 Responsive design, readable on any device

## Tech Stack

- Hugo static site generation
- AI content collection and summarization
- A clean, modern theme design
