# Privacy Policy

hackcv is an AI-powered news aggregation tool. We value your privacy.

## What We Don't Collect

- ❌ No account registration required
- ❌ No personal information (name, phone, email)
- ❌ No user tracking cookies
- ❌ No personalized ads

## Automatically Collected Information

When you visit hackcv, our servers may automatically log:

- **Access time**
- **Pages visited**
- **Browser type**
- **Approximate location (country/region level only)**

This information is used solely for site operations and analytics, and is not linked to any individual.

## AI Processing

- All content is automatically collected and summarized by AI
- We do not create user profiles
- AI-generated content is for reference only

## Third-Party Services

hackcv may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

## Changes

This policy may be updated over time. Major changes will be announced on the homepage.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues or email.
